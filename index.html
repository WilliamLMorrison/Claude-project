      About this edition

      This page reads Mistborn.xml, a hand-encoded TEI (Text Encoding Initiative) file, and renders it in the browser. Nothing in the text is retyped for the web, so changes to the XML show up here on the next load.

      • <p n="…"> becomes a numbered paragraph you can link to (for example #p-26).
      • <persName>, <roleName>, <placeName>, <orgName> and <rs> become highlighted terms, collected in the index. A key attribute groups different forms of the same term, such as “obligators” under “obligator”.
      • <note> becomes a numbered footnote. Click its number to read it in place.
      • <ref target="…"> becomes a link to the Coppermind wiki.

      The text is an excerpt from Mistborn: The Final Empire © Brandon Sanderson, published by Tor Books. It's reproduced here for a class assignment. The annotations and encoding are by William Morrison.
